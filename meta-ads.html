Program Notes

Setup Fee: A one-time fee of $1,500 CAD covers full account setup, pixel and conversion tracking configuration, UTM and reporting setup, and initial strategy and planning.

Ad Spend: Ad spend is separate from our management fee and is paid directly to Meta by your practice. This keeps your spend fully transparent and in your control. We recommend a minimum of $600 CAD per month to generate meaningful data and results.

Account Ownership: All campaigns are built inside your own Meta Business Suite and Ads Manager accounts. You retain full admin access and ownership at all times.

Minimum Commitment: All Meta Ads programs require a minimum 3-month commitment, with all fees payable through to the end of the third month. This ensures the necessary resources are allocated for campaign asset development and the effective implementation of all advertising strategies.

Cancellation: A minimum 30-day written notice is required to cancel the Meta Ads program. This applies after the initial 3-month commitment period has been fulfilled.

Pricing: Pricing is per practice. Multi-location practices are each treated as separate programs unless campaigns are identical across locations — in which case discounts may apply.
