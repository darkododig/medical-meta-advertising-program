Program Notes

Add-On Requirement: This program is available exclusively to practices actively enrolled in Wisevu's Meta Ads Management program. Cancellation of Meta Ads automatically cancels this add-on.

Pricing: $499 CAD/month per practice, all-inclusive. A one-time $350 CAD setup fee covers AI onboarding, community management configuration, and scheduling setup.

Strategy & Reporting: Content strategy and monthly performance review are covered in your existing Meta Ads monthly call. No separate strategy call is required for this program.

Platforms: Facebook and Instagram are included in the base program. Your existing monthly content can be extended to additional platforms (e.g. LinkedIn, TikTok, Pinterest, YouTube, Google Business Profile) with platform-appropriate formatting and publishing at +$49 CAD/month per platform. This covers distribution and adaptation of your existing content, not a separate content strategy for that platform.

Content Calendar Delivery: Your content calendar is delivered on the 15th of each month for the following month, giving you time to review and approve before anything goes live.

Asset & Request Submissions: Please submit any photos, clinic assets, or content requests by the 10th of each month. Original photos and content from your practice are encouraged whenever available, helping create a more authentic and distinctive social presence.

AI Community Management: Comment and DM responses are handled by an AI trained on your practice's voice, services, and FAQs. Clinical questions and anything requiring a medical response are always flagged to your team.

Multi-Location Pricing: Each practice location is billed as a separate program, unless content is identical across locations — in which case discounts may apply.
